# Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does Barkie cost?**
Three tiers, all with a 14-day free trial (card required upfront, no charge until the trial ends). See current pricing on the Billing page or the public pricing page.

**What happens if my payment fails?**
You keep read-only access to your data — you can view everything, but can't create or edit until it's resolved. Nothing is deleted. Update your payment method on the Billing page and access is restored automatically.

**Can I cancel anytime?**
Yes, from the Billing page. No lock-in contract.

**How does Barkie work out my printer's cost per hour?**
From what you enter when you add the printer: purchase cost ÷ expected lifetime hours gives depreciation per hour, plus power draw (watts) × your electricity rate gives running cost per hour. Both are baked into every Costing Template that uses that printer.

**Do I need to know my exact print weight and time?**
No — use the built-in **Slicer**. Upload your STL and it works out the real weight, filament length, and print time for you, using your actual printer/filament settings.

**Can I add team members?**
Yes, up to 3 active team members per account, each with `admin` or `sales` role. Sales-role users can't see Billing, Company/Shop Profile, or Reports.

**Is my data visible to other tenants?**
No — every tenant's data is fully isolated. The one deliberate exception is the Feature Request Board, which is a shared community list by design (you can see and vote on other shops' feature requests, but never their business data).

**What's the difference between Job Cards and Jobs?**
Job Cards are customer-facing intake tickets — what a customer dropped off/sent in, before you've costed the work. Jobs is your shop-floor status board for work that's actually underway. A Job Card can be converted into a Quote; once accepted and invoiced, the actual production work gets tracked on the Jobs board.

**Can customers see my Barkie dashboard?**
No. Customers only ever see what you send them (a quote/invoice PDF, a payment link) or your public Shop Profile page, which you control what appears on.

**What printers/materials does Barkie support?**
Any printer or filament — you add your own with their real specs and costs. There's no restricted list. (A printer-specs catalog with common models is available to pre-fill the basics when adding a new printer, so you don't have to look up every spec by hand.)

**Does dark mode affect my public shop page too?**
Yes — the theme choice is shared between the dashboard and the public site when you're on the same device/browser.

**Who do I talk to for support or feature requests?**
Use the Feature Request Board in the dashboard to suggest and vote on features. For direct support, use the contact details on the public site.
